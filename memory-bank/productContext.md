# Product Context

## Why This Exists
A PDF CV is hard to share, impossible to index, and dies in an email attachment. A personal site gives Taha one durable link to put in LinkedIn, email signatures, and applications.

## Problems It Solves
- **Discoverability** — "Taha Galata" should resolve to something he controls, not just a LinkedIn profile.
- **Context** — the CV compresses 3+ years of SAP BW/4HANA and AWS analytics work into bullets; the site can breathe.
- **Friction** — recruiters can skim on a phone and contact him without downloading anything.

## Audience
Primary: technical recruiters and hiring managers in data analytics / SAP consulting, in Turkey and internationally (he has worked with stakeholders in Germany, Spain, China, Russia).
Secondary: potential clients, professional peers.

## How It Should Work
- Land → name, title, one-line positioning, contact affordances, all above the fold.
- Scroll → experience in reverse-chronological order, then skills, education, languages.
- Contact is never more than one click away (email, LinkedIn). Phone is not published.

## Experience Goals
- **Credible over flashy.** The audience is enterprise-data people; restraint reads as competence.
- **Scannable.** Company, role, dates legible at a glance before any prose.
- **International.** English-only copy; no assumptions of Turkish context.
- **Self-contained.** No PDF escape hatch — everything a recruiter needs is on the page.
- **Honest.** Presentation upgrades the CV; it does not embellish it.
